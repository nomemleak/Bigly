<p>Bigly shows your text in huge letters, filling the entire screen of your phone. Hold it up at a concert, in a noisy room, at the airport, or to talk with someone across the street.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Fixed mode</b>: the font size adjusts itself to fill the screen. Multi-line text is supported, and words are never cut.</li>
<li><b>Scrolling mode</b> like an LED banner: adjustable speed, left to right or right to left, continuous loop.</li>
<li><b>Effects</b>, one at a time: blink, animated rainbow, shake, pulse.</li>
<li><b>History</b> of your last messages (20 by default): replay with one tap, delete one or clear everything.</li>
<li>Text and background <b>colors</b>: palette or free choice, with black on white and white on black presets.</li>
<li>Keeps the screen on and can raise brightness to the maximum while a message is shown.</li>
<li>Automatic orientation, with a button to lock portrait or landscape.</li>
<li><b>Home screen widget</b>: one tap shows your last message full screen.</li>
<li><b>Shortcuts</b> on the app icon: new message, last message, history.</li>
<li><b>Share to Bigly</b> from any app: the text fills the input field.</li>
<li><b>Emojis</b> shown huge in every mode and effect.</li>
<li><b>Export as image</b> (PNG) and share it.</li>
<li><b>13 color presets</b>, all with strong contrast: yellow on black, green on black, white on blue…</li>
<li>Material You colors on Android 12+, light, dark or system theme.</li>
<li>Respects the system "remove animations" setting.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Privacy</b>: no permissions, no network access, no ads, no trackers. Your history stays on your device.</p>
